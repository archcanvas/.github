---

**[archcanvas/archcanvas](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas)** is where the product lives in
public: ten worked examples with their source and compiled drawings, the roadmap, the changelog, the
FAQ, and the place to report a plan that came out wrong.

ArchCanvas itself is a commercial product and its application source is not open. The drawing engine
underneath — **[ArchLang](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/archlang)**, the DSL and compiler every plan
is written in — **is** open source under MIT, and stands alone.
