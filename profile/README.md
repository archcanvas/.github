## Try it

Open the studio at **[archcanvas.uk](https://archcanvas.uk)** and type a brief. A sentence is enough to start.

## Where things are

- **[archcanvas/archcanvas](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas)** is where the product lives in public: ten worked examples with their source and compiled drawings, the roadmap, the changelog and the FAQ.
- **A plan came out wrong?** Report it there. That is the place for it.
- **[ArchLang](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/archlang)** is the drawing engine underneath: the DSL and compiler every plan is written in. It is open source (MIT) and stands alone.

<sub>ArchCanvas is a commercial product and its application source is not open. Built by [Chan Meng](https://github.com/ChanMeng666).</sub>
